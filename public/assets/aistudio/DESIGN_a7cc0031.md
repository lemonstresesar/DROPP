## Brand & Style

This design system drives a mobile-first social commerce engine bridging video immersion and commercial wholesale distribution in Central Africa (Douala, Yaoundé). The visual signature fuses the kinetic velocity of short-form video feeds with the structural reliability of industrial wholesale marketplaces.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Vibrant & Kinetic:** Rapid discovery, full-bleed media, live stream pulses, and visceral tactile feedback optimized for high-refresh-rate AMOLED displays.
- **Transactional Assurance:** Authoritative, clear verification cues, high-trust payment symbology (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money), and explicit unit wholesale pricing in FCFA.
- **Street-Level Dynamism:** Direct, unpretentious commercial energy tailored for vibrant entrepreneurial hubs across Cameroon.

### Aesthetic Movement: High-Contrast Cyber-Bazaar
The aesthetic leverages absolute pitch-black backdrops to maximize OLED battery longevity and visual punch. Surfaces elevate through dark slate and charcoal containers with precise 1px hairline boundaries. Call-to-action elements erupt in high-octane saturated orange, tempered by verified trust-greens.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep AMOLED contrast, high ambient sunlight legibility in outdoor markets, and instantaneous recognition of transactional triggers.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF5A1F`):** Kinetic Solar Orange. Reserved strictly for primary transactional drives ("Commander", "Acheter en gros", "Ajouter au panier"), wholesale tiers, active video interactions, and key engagement counters.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Verified Trust Green. Signifies merchant verification badges, real-time online presence, secure escrow settlement, and MTN MoMo / Orange Money validated status.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Flash Sale / MoQ Alert Amber. Used for low inventory alerts, group buying thresholds, and Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) milestones.
- **Neutral Core (`#000000`, `#0A0A0A`, `#141414`, `#1E1E1E`, `#2A2A2A`):** Pure obsidian canvas `#000000` with layered dark neutral containers that prevent pure white bleed while maintaining edge separation.

### Text & Media Overlays
- **Primary Text (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast absolute white for prices, merchant titles, and primary headers.
- **Secondary Text (`#B0B0B0`):** Neutral gray for specifications, unit counts, and metadata.
- **Subdued Text (`#707070`):** Minor captions, timestamps, and input placeholders.
- **Overlay scrims:** Linear gradients `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)` pinned to bottom video feeds ensuring typography stays bulletproof against volatile video backgrounds.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Space Grotesk** for numerical metrics, currency tags, headlines, and compact action triggers, with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for multi-line wholesale specs, supplier descriptions, and live chat streams.

### Commercial & Numerical Styling
- **FCFA Pricing Hierarchy:** Always set in `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures where possible (`tnum`). The currency suffix `FCFA` should be set at 70% the scale of the integer value and placed with a non-breaking space (e.g., **25.000** <small>FCFA</small>).
- **Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ):** Expressed using `label-sm` in bold uppercase pill containers to ensure grossiste conditions are readable within 2 seconds of video consumption.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is mobile-first, supporting fluid video viewports with dense, Alibaba-inspired transactional modules.

### Grid & Adaptation
- **Mobile Feed (Vertical Video Viewport):** Zero-margin outer canvas during video viewing modes. The live HUD (heads-up display) employs an internal edge inset of `0.75rem` (`margin-mobile`) from bottom navigation bars and right-side interactive stacks.
- **Wholesale Marketplace & B2B Catalogs:** 2-column asymmetric fluid grid on mobile devices (`gutter: 0.75rem`), shifting to a 4-column structure on tablets, and an 8-column layout on wide screens.
- **Safe Area Defenses:** Absolute adherence to mobile safe bottom area buffers (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 64px`) to preserve interaction zones above the navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not depend on diffuse light-source drop shadows, which muddy dark interfaces. Instead, the design system utilizes surface tone shifts, ambient black-glow diffusion, and razor-sharp border framing.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas):** `#000000` (Pure AMOLED Black, media canvases).
- **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Bottom Sheets):** `#0A0A0A` to `#141414` with a `1px` outer hairline border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action Trays & Dialogs):** `#1E1E1E` with subtle ambient ring `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Tooltips):** `#2A2A2A` bordered with `rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.25)`.

### Glassmorphism & Translucent HUD
- Overlaid product detail drawers on active video streams use `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` over an alpha layer of `rgba(14, 14, 14, 0.82)`. This anchors contextual awareness of the video playing behind the B2B checkout drawer.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric, full-radius outer curves (`roundedness: 2` defaulting to `0.5rem`, reaching `1rem` on cards, and `9999px` for tactile buttons).

### Geometric Application
- **Interactive Action Buttons:** High-density rounded pills (`rounded-full`) for high-thumb reach touch targets ("Commander", "Négocier").
- **Product & Supplier Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to keep the interface structured, compact, and content-rich.
- **Live Video Avatars & Badges:** Circular `rounded-full` frames accompanied by 2px verified rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transactional ("Commander" / "Acheter en gros"):** Massive pill shape (`rounded-full`), height `52px` on mobile, background `#FF5A1F`, text `#FFFFFF` using `label-lg`. Pressed state applies a `scale(0.97)` spring transition and a color shift to `#E04B16`.
- **Secondary B2B Action ("Négocier" / "Demander un échantillon"):** Pill shape, dark slate surface `#1E1E1E`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Floating Video Action Buttons (Sidebar):** Vertical stack containing 44px circular nodes (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.6)` backdrop blur) housing monochrome glyphs for likes, comments, wholesale quotes, and share, accompanied by micro-counter labels underneath.

### Badges & Wholesale Chips
- **MoQ & Tier Chips:** Background `#1E1E1E`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.3)`, text `#FF5A1F` in `label-sm` (e.g., "Min. 10 pcs").
- **Payment & Trust Badges:** Surface tinted with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #10B981`, containing verified tick icon and carrier identity markers (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) in pure white and green.

### Mobile Navigation Bar
- Fixed at viewport bottom with height `64px` plus safe-inset.
- Background `#0A0A0A` with a top border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- 5 nodes: **Accueil** (Video discovery), **Marché** (B2B wholesale category browser), **Bouton Central '+'** (Elevated pill in `#FF5A1F` with a white plus sign, invoking live streaming or product drops), **Messages** (Chat with active red/green unread indicators), and **Profil** (Account & wholesale order tracking).

### Product Cards (Marché / Grid View)
- Dark container `#141414` with a 1:1 media thumbnail at the top.
- Integrated dual-pricing display: Single unit price in secondary text, crossed with high-emphasis wholesale price in `price-card` (`Space Grotesk`, `#FFFFFF`).
- Lower card sector displays supplier badge, Douala/Yaoundé warehouse indicator, and direct 1-tap cart addition button.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Fields
- Background `#141414`, active focus border `1.5px solid #FF5A1F`.
- Placeholders set in `#707070`.
- Native keyboard triggers configured for numerical input when dealing with FCFA offers and quantity adjustments.
